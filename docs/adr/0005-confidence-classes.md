# 0005: Classify confidence and reference-only rules

Label each rule assessment direct, medium or low confidence according to how closely its available inputs and proxy represent the published rule, and identify rules used only for reference at a site. Show these labels and approximation limits with the results; include only direct and medium, non-reference rules in the combined member union. Rules that cannot be evaluated remain distinct from clear conditions, and LLCC rules at sites without evidence of their application are reference-only.
