# 0008: Keep three evaluations separate

Report the retrospective proxy check on near-analysis data, the retrospective deterministic surface forecast check at fixed leads, and the forward audit of ledgered ensemble forecasts as three separate evaluations, each with its own sample size and scope. They answer different questions about rule proxies, surface forecasts and the actual ensemble board, so their metrics cannot be blended into one performance claim. Official concerns are another forecast rather than observed violations, and agreement with them must be labelled accordingly.
