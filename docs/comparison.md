# How Scrubcast compares

Checked on 2026-11-10. Each of these does something Scrubcast does not.

| | What it tells you | What it asks of you | Where it is ahead of Scrubcast |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 45th Weather Squadron launch forecast | One probability of violating weather constraints and the concerns behind it, for Florida launches | Nothing | It is the official forecast, made by forecasters with radar, field mills and observations no model grid has |
| [stagesep](https://stagesep.com/) | A "live weather-GO assessment for the next SpaceX Starship launch window", hour by hour, per commit criterion | Nothing stated on the page | More inputs (the NWS pad forecast, HRRR, upper-level winds), a refresh every 10 minutes, and depth at Starbase, where Scrubcast's rules are reference only |
| [Windy Premium](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/windy-com-weather-radar/id1161387262) | Forecasts from many models for any place; its Premium section lists 1-hour data, a 15-day outlook with 4 to 6 updates a day, and model comparison | A subscription | Maps, radar and many more models, anywhere. It has no launch rules |
| [RocketLaunch.live Premium](https://www.rocketlaunch.live/premium) | Launch schedules; Premium adds calendar and RSS feeds, push and email notifications and API access | A pay-what-you-wish subscription | Notifications, launch changelogs and statistics across providers. Its Premium page does not mention weather |
| [Flighty Pro](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/flighty-live-flight-tracker/id1358823008) | "Delay predictions, including the reason why", for airline flights | A subscription | The same idea, the reason behind a delay, delivered as pilot-grade FAA delay alerts. It covers flights, not launches |
| Scrubcast | Which rule the forecast models break, member by member and hour by hour, beside the official number, for supported scheduled orbital launches in the next ten days, with the fullest coverage at Tier A pads | Nothing for the site; uv or pipx for the terminal board | |

The 45th Weather Squadron's site refused requests from this network on 2026-11-10, so its row describes the fields Launch Library 2 mirrors from it (`probability` and `weather_concerns`), which Scrubcast reads.
